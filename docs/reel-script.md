# Astral Armory: reel script (75 s)

Record in Roblox Studio with OBS at 1920×1080, 60 fps. Open `AstralArmory.rbxl` and press Play.

## Before you record

- Hide the Studio chrome: Studio full screen (F11) or crop the OBS source to the game view.
- Type `/lvl 10` in chat for the shots that need level 10 and the boss.
- Socket the gems from the bag (G) before the gem shot: Băng (frost) on Q.
- The OBS scene needs only game audio, or no audio, and music added in the edit.
- Record each shot as its own clip with 2 s of spare footage on both sides, then cut to the times below.
- Camera: right-drag to orbit, mouse wheel to zoom. "Low" means the camera near the floor looking up.

## Tips

- Do the boss shots with full Energy. Cast T just before B so the fight uses the radiant variants.
- If the knights get in the way of a skill shot, wait 8 s for them to respawn in their rings, then pull a
  group of 3 or 4.
- The boss has 4,000 HP. A full fight at level 10 takes about two minutes, so keep the best 17 s.
- For the passive tree shot, press Đặt lại (Reset) first so the path starts from the center.
